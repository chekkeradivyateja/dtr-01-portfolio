Privacy & legal

Short version: this site collects nothing. No cookies, no JavaScript, no analytics, no forms, and nothing loaded from anyone else’s servers.

Privacy

This site is a set of static files. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no scripts and embeds no third-party content. The fonts are self-hosted, so even font requests never leave this domain. There is nothing here that could identify you, and nothing I could sell.

The host, Vercel Inc., processes basic request data such as IP address, user agent and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, as any web server does. That processing is governed by Vercel’s privacy policy. I add no logging or analytics of my own on top of it.

My email address, phone number and résumé are published here on purpose, so recruiters can reach me. If you email or call me, I use your details only to reply to you, and I don’t share them with anyone.

Other sites

Links to my other projects (VereChek, DV Verification Hub, Notion Student Hub) and to GitHub, LinkedIn and Gumroad take you to sites with their own privacy policies. Some of them use analytics or payment processors; this one doesn’t.

Accuracy

Everything on the datasheet describes my own work. Figures come from my own records — repositories, commit history, published sites and my résumé — and are current as of the revision date. Screenshots were captured from the live sites on 30 September 2026. If you spot something that’s out of date, I’d like to know.

Employer

This is a personal site. The opinions and personal projects here are my own and are not made on behalf of, or endorsed by, my employer. Descriptions of work done at Maven Silicon stay at the level of detail in my public résumé.

Accessibility

This site targets WCAG 2.2 level AA. It works with a keyboard alone, with screen readers, at 400% zoom, in light and dark modes, and it stops all animation when your system asks for reduced motion. If anything doesn’t work for you, email me and I’ll fix it.
